# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version. You're a mage duelling in the Undercroft, an arena deep underground.
- Swear an oath to one of seven mage lords: Ignis the Reckless Pyromancer, Lumi the Guided Alchemist, Morrigan the Blight Weaver, Orin the Iron Sage, Ren the Arctic Witch, Sable the Void Whisperer or Volta the Storm Conductor. Your oath binds you to their element: only their cards and plain cards will answer you.
- Each lord has an oath power you can use once a turn, and their own way to win: Fire burns hard and burns you too, Light heals and gilds, Blight rots, Iron armors up, Ice freezes and shatters, Void drains and echoes, Storm casts fast and chains.
- Brewing: reagents go into your cauldron and wait. Your next spell pours them all in and gets every one of their effects. A full cauldron of 3 draws you a card.
- 17 recipes to discover. The right three reagents together make something extra happen, and found recipes are kept in your Grimoire.
- The gauntlet: 5 pits against rival mages, ending with a champion who plays by a rule of their own. After each win, take a card for your satchel. You have 3 lives.
- Win the champion's pit for your lord's trophy. Seven trophies to collect.
- Every card is drawn in ink. The elements are told apart by their patterns, and card kinds by the shape of their art: familiars square, spells arched, reagents round.
- Your gauntlet is saved as you play, even mid-duel, and the game can be added to a phone's home screen like the other Ink games.
