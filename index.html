Ink Craft

Swear an oath. Brew spells. Get trophies.

Play
  • Seven mage lords. Swear to one and you’re bound to their element.
  • Brew your spells. Stir reagents into your cauldron and pour them into a spell. Find the recipes.
  • Win the Undercroft. Duel through five pits to the champion for each lord’s trophy.
